##  📌 Project Overview

Hospital admissions data plays a critical role in understanding operational performance, resource utilization, and financial outcomes. This project analyzes admissions data for a fictional healthcare client, Greenwood Hospital, to identify trends in patient volume, readmissions, and treatment costs and department level performance.

Using Power BI, the dashboard was designed to simulate an executive-level reporting tool that provides leadership with actionable insights into hospital operations. The analysis focuses on how data can support operational planning, cost management, and data driven decision making.

---

## 🎯 Business Question

How can hospital admissions data be used to identify readmission trends, resource utilization patterns, and operational areas impacting hospital performance and financial planning?

Hospital leadership needs a clear view of:

- patient admission trends over time
- departments with elevated readmission activity
- drivers of treatment costs and resource utilization
- payer segments contributing the highest revenue
- operational patterns impacting staffing and capacity planning

Without centralized reporting and dashboard visibility, these operational insights are more difficult to monitor and act on effectively.

 ##  Data Preparation

The dataset was prepared and modeled within Power BI to support reporting, trend analysis, and executive level dashboard development.

Key preparation steps included:

- Importing and validating structured admissions data
- Creating calculated measures using DAX for:
   - Total Revenue
   - Average Length of Stay
   - Readmission Rate
   - Patient Satisfaction Metrics
- Structuring relationships between patient, department, and insurance and admissions data
- Preparing fields for time based trend analysis and department level aggregation
- Standardizing metrics for KPI reporting and dashboard visualization

The cleaned and modeled dataset was then used to build an interactive Power BI dashboard for operational reporting and analysis.

    >Note: The dataset used in this project is synthetic and intended for analytical demonstration purposes
    only.

##  📊 Key Insights
### Readmission Trends
Emergency and Cardiology departments showed the highest readmission rates, indicating potential gaps in discharge planning, follow up care, or patient monitoring.

### Resource Utilization
Oncology generated the highest treatment costs and longest average length of stay, making it a major driver of hospital resource utilization and financial impact.

### Admissions Volume Trends
Patient admissions fluctuated throughout the year, highlighting opportunities for improved staffing allocation and capacity planning.

### Revenue & Payer Analysis
Medicare and private insurance accounted for the majority of treatment revenue, identifying key payer groups contributing to hospital financial performance.

### Overall Trend
Hospital utilization and financial impact were concentrated within specific departments and payer groups rather than evenly distributed across the organization.

---

## Analyst Interpretation

The analysis suggests that hospital operational performance varies significantly across departments, particularly in areas related to readmissions, treatment costs, and patient utilization.

Higher readmission rates within Emergency and Cardiology may indicate opportunities to improve discharge coordination, patient follow up, and care transition processes. Additionally, Oncology’s high treatment costs and longer patient stays highlight the importance of resource planning and operational monitoring within high utilization departments.

By analyzing admissions data, healthcare organizations can better understand operational trends, support financial planning, and improve decision making related to staffing, patient care, and hospital performance.

---

## Strategic Recommendations
1. Strengthen discharge planning and follow up processes within departments with elevated readmission activity.
2. Monitor Oncology utilization trends to support resource allocation and cost management strategies.
3. Use admissions trend reporting to improve staffing and hospital capacity planning.
4. Expand executive dashboard reporting to support ongoing operational monitoring and performance analysis.
5. Continue tracking payer utilization patterns to support financial forecasting and reimbursement planning.

---

## Assumptions & Limitations
### Assumptions
- Admissions and treatment data accurately represented hospital operational activity within the dataset.
- Readmission metrics reflected valid patient return activity within the reporting period.
- Department level analysis provided meaningful operational insight into utilization and performance trends.
  
### Limitations
- The dataset was synthetic and created for portfolio analysis purposes.
- The analysis focused on descriptive operational reporting rather than predictive modeling.
- Additional clinical severity, diagnosis, and staffing data could provide deeper operational insight.
- Real world hospital operations may involve additional external and seasonal factors not represented within
  the dataset.
